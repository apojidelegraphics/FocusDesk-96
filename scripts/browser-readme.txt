FOCUSDESK 96 - BROWSER VERSION (saved to your computer)
=========================================================

This folder is the BROWSER version of FocusDesk 96 (version __VERSION__),
downloaded to your computer. It is NOT the desktop app.

HOW TO OPEN IT
  1. Keep all the files in this folder together.
  2. Open "index.html" in Google Chrome (double-click it, or drag it onto a
     Chrome window). Edge, Brave and Firefox work too.
  3. It runs offline. No install, no account.

WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
  The browser version keeps your data inside your web browser's own storage,
  not as files in this folder. That means:
    - Clearing your browsing data / site data can erase it.
    - A different browser, or a different browser profile, starts empty.
  Protect yourself: open Control Panel and use "Save backup file" regularly.
  Use "Restore from file" to bring a backup back.

WANT YOUR DATA AS REAL FILES ON YOUR COMPUTER?
  Get the desktop app (Mac, Windows, Linux). It saves everything as plain files,
  makes automatic daily backups, and works with Dropbox or iCloud folders:
  https://github.com/apojidelegraphics/FocusDesk-96/releases/latest

You can move a backup between the browser version and the desktop app.
